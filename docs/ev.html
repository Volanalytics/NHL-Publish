PROD is the canonical production blended model. Each L82/L20/L10 window independently rebuilds expected-lineup player scoring from up to that player's trailing NHL games, aggregates the game projection, and reprices the same market. L82 may bridge the prior season. Short-history and zero-history handling is carried in projection provenance.
